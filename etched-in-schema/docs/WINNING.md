# How to win this hackathon

Ten priorities for the demo and submission. When in doubt, choose whatever
makes the golden path more reliable or more verifiable.

1. **Unforgettable first 30 seconds.** The avatar speaks the exact intro
   script, then immediately does one real thing, with its receipt appearing on
   screen. Show proof before explaining anything.

2. **Let a judge verify something themselves.** Hand them a receipt ID or
   on-chain anchor and a one-step way to check it (link or QR code). "Don't
   trust me, check it" is the thesis made physical.

3. **Show one refusal live.** Ask the identity to do something outside its
   OSCAL boundary; the bridge refuses with the exact missing capability. An
   agent that *can't* misbehave beats one that doesn't.

4. **Use Meta's model visibly and well.** Show Muse Spark doing the reasoning
   (planning the business, mapping requirements to controls), say so on
   screen, and let it make one real tool call through the identity's mount.

5. **Protect the golden path above everything.** Intro → "create a CMS for this
   business" → provision → receipts → anchor. Run it end to end daily from
   Day 5. If it does not pass three times in a row, add nothing new.

6. **Redaction is the "wow" moment.** Content with a name, email and phone
   number is published and masked automatically, with the receipt naming the
   policy. Everyone understands it instantly.

7. **A fallback for every live dependency.** Record a full run and know the
   exact step at which you would switch to it. Model, chain or Wi-Fi can fail
   on stage; a calm switch still scores.

8. **Tell a story with a real business.** One believable small business (e.g.
   a Fort Lauderdale firm that needs a compliant client portal), with a name
   and a problem, solved by the identity.

9. **Say the positioning line out loud.** *"Workload identity like SPIFFE
   proves who is calling. Etched in Schema also proves what it was allowed to
   do — and what it actually did."*

10. **Ship the evidence, not just the demo.** Public repo with a README that
    separates new from pre-existing work, a short video, a one-page
    architecture diagram, and a live link judges can open later.

**Bonus: freeze scope on Day 7.** After that, only polish, rehearse and fix.
